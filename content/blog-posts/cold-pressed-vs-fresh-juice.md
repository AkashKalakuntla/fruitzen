Cold pressed juice is made by slowly pressing fruit, while fresh juice is usually made in a fast-spinning juicer or blender. Both start with whole fruit. Cold pressed juice tends to be smoother with brighter colour and costs a little more. FruitZen, a juice bar in Kukatpally, Hyderabad, serves both side by side: fresh juices from ₹50 and cold pressed juices from ₹60, all made to order.

## How Is Cold Pressed Juice Made?

A cold press machine crushes fruit and then presses it under pressure to release the juice. There is no high-speed blade spinning through the fruit. The process is slower, and it usually takes more fruit to fill a glass.

At FruitZen in Kukatpally, cold pressed juices are pressed when you order. FruitZen's list is short: Mosambi, Pineapple, Orange (Imported Malta) and Pomegranate. All four have no added sugar.

## How Is Regular Fresh Juice Made?

Most juice shops in Hyderabad make fresh juice with a centrifugal juicer or a mixer-blender. A fast blade breaks the fruit and spins out the juice in seconds. It is quick and affordable, which is why it is the standard at local juice centres.

FruitZen's fresh juices are also made to order from fruit sourced every morning. The range is wider: 19 fresh juices from Banana (₹50) to Pomegranate and Avocado (₹120).

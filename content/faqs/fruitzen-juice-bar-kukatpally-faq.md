## Where Is FruitZen Located in Kukatpally?

FruitZen is at Shop No 1, H.No 4-42-950, near the HP Petrol Pump, Ramarao Nagar Colony, Paparayadu Nagar, Kukatpally, Hyderabad, Telangana 500072. The shop is a short ride from KPHB Colony and JNTU. Search "FruitZen Kukatpally" on Google Maps for directions, or call +91 91117 36777 if you cannot find the entrance.

## What Time Does FruitZen Open and Close?

FruitZen opens at 9 am and closes at 11 pm, seven days a week. That makes it one of the later-closing juice bars in the area, useful after evening gym sessions or college. Special-day timings, such as festivals, are posted on FruitZen's Google Business Profile.

## Is FruitZen the Best Juice Shop in Kukatpally?

FruitZen holds a 5.0★ Google rating, and reviewers mention fruit quality and freshness. One wrote: "The fruit quality is amazing, and you can actually taste the freshness." - ABC (Apple, Beetroot & Carrot): ₹80
- Cold pressed juices: ₹60 (Mosambi) to ₹150 (Pomegranate)

- Mojitos: ₹70 each
- Cold coffees: ₹99–₹129

## Does FruitZen Serve Cold Pressed Juice?

Yes. FruitZen serves four cold pressed juices: Mosambi (₹60), Pineapple (₹120), Orange from Imported Malta (₹149) and Pomegranate (₹150). They are 100% natural with no added sugar. Read more on [cold pressed juice in Kukatpally](/resources/cold-pressed-juice-kukatpally).

## Does FruitZen Add Sugar or Ice?

FruitZen's cold pressed juices have no added sugar. For fresh juices, smoothies and shakes, ask at the counter for no sugar or no ice, and FruitZen will make it that way because every drink is made to order.

FruitZen serves waffles (₹129–₹199), momos (₹99–₹159), fries (₹79–₹99), Maggi (₹59–₹89), fresh fruit bowls (₹70–₹150) and value combos from ₹129. See [waffles in Kukatpally](/resources/waffles-in-kukatpally) for the full waffle list.

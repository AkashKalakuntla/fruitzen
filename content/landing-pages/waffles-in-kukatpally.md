## What Is the Best Waffle to Try First at FruitZen?

For chocolate lovers, the Nutella waffle (₹169) is the safe first pick. The Lotus Biscoff (₹199) is the most indulgent. If you want something lighter, the FruitZen Mixed Fruit waffle (₹189) is topped with fresh fruit from the same morning delivery used for FruitZen's juices.

## What Drink Goes Best with a Waffle?

A tangy juice balances a sweet waffle. Try a cold pressed Pineapple (₹120) or a Watermelon juice (₹50). If you prefer coffee, FruitZen's cold coffees run from ₹99 (Classic) to ₹129 (Caramel Cream).
